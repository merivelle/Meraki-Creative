A quick tip on copying: open a kit page, then click just before "Meraki" at the top of the white box and drag to the very end of the content to select it, and copy (Cmd + C). That grabs the design without the instruction bar.

1. Apple Mail signature (Mac)

  1. Open the signature and copy the box (select it, then Cmd + C).
  2. In Apple Mail: Mail menu → Settings (older macOS: Preferences) → Signatures tab.
  3. In the left column, click your Bluehost email account. Click the + button to add a signature and name it "Meraki".
  4. Important: uncheck "Always match my default message font" (below the signature box) so the formatting is kept.
  5. Click into the signature box, select the placeholder text it created, and paste (Cmd + V). Your branded signature appears.
  6. At the bottom, set the "Choose Signature" popup to "Meraki" so it is used automatically on new emails.

2. Apple Mail reusable templates

The easy way (always works): when you need to send one, open the template page, copy the box, click New Message in Mail, and paste. Fill the [brackets] and send. Keep this kit bookmarked.

To save it as a one-click template (Stationery):

  1. Click New Message, paste a template into the body.
  2. File menu → Save as Stationery… → give it a name (e.g. "Quote").
  3. To reuse: New Message → click the Stationery button (top right of the new message, the photo/grid icon) → Custom → pick your template. Fill the [brackets] and send.

3. Bluehost webmail signature (Roundcube)

  1. Log in to your Bluehost webmail (Roundcube).
  2. Settings → Identities → click your email address.
  3. Turn on HTML signature (the toggle by the signature box; or click the <> source button).
  4. Open the signature, copy the box, paste it in, and click Save.

4. Bluehost webmail templates (Roundcube)

  1. Click Compose. Make sure the editor is in HTML mode (toggle near the top of the compose window, sometimes under the "..." options).
  2. Open a template, copy the box, and paste it into the message body.
  3. Fill the [brackets] and send. (Optional: save plain-text versions under Settings → Responses for quick reuse.)
Send yourself a test email after installing the signature to make sure spacing and links look right on mobile.

Optional: if you ever use Gmail

Signature: Gmail Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, paste, then Save Changes. Templates: enable Settings → Advanced → Templates, paste a template into Compose, then three dots → Templates → Save draft as template. Reuse from that same Templates menu.
