( 01 ) For Actors

For Actors

A complete, castable presence — the website, the reel, and the paperwork — built to the standard your reps and casting directors expect.

01

Actor Websites

A clean, fast, mobile-ready site that puts your reel, headshots, resume, and contact in one place. Designed to be the single link you drop on Backstage, Spotlight, Casting Networks, and in every email signature — and to make you look like you take this seriously.

02

Demo Reel Editing

Your strongest work, cut to lead. We sequence your clips for momentum, open on a moment that holds, and keep it tight — the kind of reel that gets watched to the end instead of skipped at ten seconds. Color and sound balanced across sources.

03

Scene & Self-Tape Editing

Individual scenes and self-tapes cleaned up, trimmed, and balanced so the performance — not the production — is what reads. Ideal for adding fresh material to your reel, your site, or a specific submission.

04

Resume Formatting

An industry-standard acting resume that's easy to scan in five seconds: credits organized correctly, training and skills where they belong, and a layout that matches your headshot and website. Print-ready and digital.

05

Casting Material Organization

Headshots, reels, sizes, links, and contact details organized into one clean, share-ready package — so when a rep or casting office asks for "everything," you send one tidy link instead of scrambling.

( 02 ) For Filmmakers

For Filmmakers

The sites and materials that make directors, producers, and companies look as ambitious as their slate — to financiers, festivals, and collaborators.

01

Director Websites

A work-first portfolio that lets your films lead and gets out of the way. Built to send to reps, financiers, and festival programmers — credible, current, and easy to update as your reel grows.

02

Production Company Websites

A proper home for your slate, team, and contact. The page that tells a financier or distributor you're a real operation — clear about what you make and who you are, without the corporate stock-photo gloss.

03

Pitch Deck Design

Look, tone, logline, comps, and team designed to actually sell the project. A deck that carries the mood of the film and reads cleanly in a meeting or an inbox — the difference between "interesting" and "let's talk."

04

Film Websites

A dedicated, cinematic page for a single film or series: trailer, stills, synopsis, credits, and press in one place. Built for festival runs, distribution conversations, and audience launches.

05

Trailer & Social Clip Editing

Trailers, teasers, and vertical cutdowns edited to travel — from festival submission requirements to scroll-stopping clips for Instagram and TikTok. Paced for the platform, cut to make people want the whole thing.
