# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Actors and filmmakers who need to present their existing work professionally online.

- **Actors** — from early-career to working professionals. Context: sending a single link to
  agents/managers, casting directors, and casting platforms (Backstage, Spotlight, Casting
  Networks). The job: look castable and serious so the right people lean in.
- **Filmmakers** — directors and production companies. Context: sending a portfolio or pitch to
  reps, financiers, festival programmers, and collaborators. The job: look as ambitious as their
  slate so a project gets taken seriously.

They are taste-literate and immersed in film/theatre culture. They can instantly smell a generic
template or a cheap service. The emotional goal: **confidence and credibility** — "this person
takes their craft seriously, and so should I."

## Product Purpose

**Positioning (June 2026): "A creative studio for storytellers."** Meraki Creative (a boutique
studio by Merivelle) is NOT an actor-only / reel-editing / marketing / website-design business.
It is a boutique creative studio across **three co-equal pillars**:
- **Post-Production** — short film, trailer, teaser, demo reel, scene, and social editing.
- **Digital Presence** — actor, director, production-company, portfolio, and film websites.
- **Creative Materials** — pitch decks and lookbooks.
Actors + filmmakers stay as audiences, not the whole identity. Core message: **"The work is
already there. We help present it."** Reference feel: A24 creative studio / editorial design
studio / boutique post-production house. The site is the portfolio; success = qualified
"Start Your Project" inquiries.

## Brand Personality

Elevated · cinematic · personal. Warm, clear, and industry-aware — a knowledgeable insider, not
a vendor. Confident without shouting. The name comes from *meraki*: doing something with soul,
creativity, and love.

## Anti-references

- **Cheap reel-editing services / Fiverr-tier gig pages** — busy, badge-stuffed, discount-coded.
- **Generic marketing agencies** — corporate gradients, stock-photo teams, buzzword decks.
- **Techy SaaS landing pages** — neon, dark-glass, feature-grid, hero-metric template.
- **Overly feminine / wedding-blogger aesthetics** — soft scripts everywhere, pastel florals.
- **The saturated 2026 "editorial-typographic AI" look** — cream body bg + tiny mono eyebrows on
  every section + 01/02/03 numbered markers + identical card grids + no real imagery. (The
  current build leans here; departing it tastefully is an explicit goal.)

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the showreel.** Every choice must prove craft, not just describe it. Show, don't tell.
2. **Cinematic over corporate.** It should feel like it belongs in film and theatre — never like a generic agency.
3. **The work leads; design frames it.** Imagery and the client's material are the heroes; the design gets out of the way at the right moments.
4. **Personal, not factory.** One human (Merivelle) with real taste — warmth and specificity over scale and sameness.
5. **Practical and fast.** Clear path to inquiry; nothing precious gets in the way of "Start Your Project."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text contrast ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 (raise muted greys toward ink if close).
- Full keyboard navigation; visible focus states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — every animation needs a reduced/crossfade fallback.
- Content fully visible without JavaScript; motion is enhancement only.
