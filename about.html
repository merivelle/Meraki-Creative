( 01 ) Who's behind it

Hi, I'm Merivelle.

Meraki Creative started from a simple frustration: so much good work by actors and filmmakers gets overlooked, not because the talent isn't there, but because it isn't presented well. A great performance buried in a clumsy reel. A serious filmmaker with a website that undercuts them. A strong project let down by a flat deck.

I come from — and care deeply about — the film and acting world, so I build everything the way I'd want my own materials built: clean, cinematic, and made to meet the actual standards of the people deciding your next opportunity. No templates dressed up as custom work. No content-mill sameness.

This is a small studio on purpose. You work with me directly, your project gets real attention, and every choice is made for your specific goals.

— Merivelle

( 02 ) How I work

A few things that stay true on every project.

01

Industry-aware, always

I build to the conventions your reps, casting offices, festivals, and financiers actually expect — so your materials read as professional before a word is spoken.

02

Cinematic over corporate

Everything is designed to feel like it belongs in film and theatre: editorial, restrained, warm. Your work leads; the design gets out of the way.

03

Clear, kind, and on time

Honest scope, plain-language updates, and turnarounds that respect your deadlines. You'll always know what's happening and what's next.

04

Your soul, not mine

The goal is never to make it look "like me." It's to make it unmistakably you — sharpened, framed, and ready for the room.
